## 1. Purpose

This policy defines Northstar Analytics' performance review cycle, participant responsibilities, ratings, and exception process. Reviews are intended to support clear expectations, useful feedback, and consistent development decisions.

## 2. Scope

It applies to regular employees unless People Operations records a different cycle for a role or employment status. It does not establish a promise of promotion, compensation, or continued employment.

## 3. Definitions

- **Review cycle** means the annual period ending on December 31.
- **Self-review** means the employee's written reflection on outcomes, behaviors, and development.
- **Manager review** means the manager's documented assessment and rating.
- **Calibration** means the confidential process used to review rating consistency across teams.

## 4. Policy Rules

### PR-001 — Annual Cycle

Northstar conducts one formal performance review for each eligible employee during the annual cycle. People Operations publishes the schedule and may set earlier internal deadlines.

### PR-002 — Self-Review

Employees must submit a self-review within 10 business days after the self-review window opens. The self-review should address agreed goals, results, collaboration, and development needs.

### PR-003 — Manager Review

Managers must complete the manager review within 10 business days after receiving the employee's self-review or after the self-review deadline passes, whichever is earlier. The review must include a rating and evidence-based comments.

### PR-004 — Review Conversation

The manager must discuss the review with the employee before the review is finalized. The employee may record comments or disagreement in the designated system.

### PR-005 — Rating Scale

Managers must use the four-level rating scale published by People Operations: Does Not Meet, Partially Meets, Meets, and Exceeds Expectations. Managers must not invent an additional rating level.

### PR-006 — Calibration

People Operations may conduct calibration before finalization. Calibration may recommend consistency changes, but the manager remains responsible for explaining the final rating to the employee.

### PR-007 — Probationary and New Employees

An employee who starts after October 1 may receive a documented check-in instead of a formal annual rating for that cycle. People Operations determines the applicable treatment based on start date and role.

### PR-008 — Review Records

The final review and employee comments must be stored in the designated system. Managers must not store performance reviews in personal files or share them with unauthorized people.

## 5. Exceptions

People Operations may adjust a review deadline for approved leave, a manager change, an extended absence, or a documented system issue. The adjusted deadline must be recorded. An employee may request a review of process fairness within 10 business days after finalization.

## 6. Procedures

People Operations opens the cycle and sends deadlines. Employees submit self-reviews. Managers write and discuss reviews, then submit them for calibration when required. The employee records comments, and People Operations closes the record after finalization.

## 7. Responsibilities

Reviews should describe observable work and agreed expectations rather than personal assumptions. Employees may provide context or disagreement without changing the manager's responsibility to complete a review. Managers should raise a deadline concern as soon as it becomes known so People Operations can determine whether an adjustment is appropriate. Review records should be handled as confidential personnel information and shared only through authorized processes.

Employees provide honest self-assessments and participate in discussion. Managers set expectations, gather evidence, complete reviews, and protect confidentiality. People Operations owns the cycle, scale, calibration, records, and exceptions.
