## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how employees request, use, and document vacation and sick leave at Northstar Analytics. It establishes predictable notice, approval, and absence-notification practices while allowing reasonable handling of unexpected events.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to regular full-time and part-time employees. Contractors and agency workers follow the terms of their engagement unless a contract says otherwise. Leave balances and payroll treatment are maintained in the company HR system.

## 3. Definitions

- **Vacation leave** means planned time away for rest, personal matters, or other non-medical reasons.
- **Sick leave** means time away because of illness, medical care, or recovery.
- **Restricted period** means a team-specific period published by a manager when staffing needs limit vacation approvals.
- **Business day** means Monday through Friday excluding Northstar company holidays.

## 4. Policy Rules

### LV-001 — Vacation Eligibility

Employees may request vacation after their start date. Requests are subject to available balance, team coverage, and manager approval. Approval of one request does not guarantee approval of later requests.

### LV-002 — Vacation Approval

Managers must approve or decline a complete vacation request in the HR system. Employees must not treat a request as approved until the system records the manager decision.

### LV-003 — Vacation Advance Notice

Employees must submit vacation requests at least 10 business days before the first requested day. A manager may approve a shorter notice period when coverage is adequate.

### LV-004 — Sick Leave Notification

Employees who cannot work because of illness must notify their manager before their scheduled start time when reasonably possible. The notice may be sent through the normal team channel or another agreed contact method.

### LV-005 — Unexpected Absence

When advance notice is not reasonably possible, the employee must notify the manager as soon as practical and provide an expected return date if known. Failure to provide notice may be treated as an attendance concern.

### LV-006 — Documentation

People Operations may request appropriate documentation for extended or recurring sick leave. Employees must submit requested documentation within the time stated in the request; this policy does not prescribe a particular medical form.

### LV-007 — Restricted Periods

A manager may designate a restricted period for a documented business need. The manager must publish the dates and reason to the affected team before applying the restriction. Vacation requests during a restricted period may be declined even when the employee has an available balance.

## 5. Exceptions

A manager may approve shorter vacation notice for coverage reasons under LV-003. People Operations may authorize an exception to notice or documentation timing for a serious medical event, protected leave process, or other documented circumstance. The exception decision must be recorded in the HR system. Restricted periods do not override an approved protected-leave process.

## 6. Procedures

Employees submit vacation requests in the HR system, identify the requested dates, and wait for the recorded manager decision. For sick leave, employees notify their manager and update the expected return date when it changes. Managers review coverage before deciding vacation requests. People Operations resolves disputed leave records and maintains balances.

## 7. Responsibilities

Leave planning should account for handoffs, customer commitments, and known team coverage needs. An employee should provide enough context for a manager to evaluate coverage without disclosing unnecessary medical information. A manager's decision should be based on coverage and policy requirements, not on informal preferences that are applied inconsistently. People Operations may ask the manager and employee to correct an incomplete record before resolving a dispute.

Employees are responsible for accurate requests, timely notification, and truthful leave records. Managers are responsible for prompt decisions, consistent coverage review, and communicating restricted periods. People Operations is responsible for records, balances, and exception documentation.
