## 1. Purpose

This policy defines which business expenses Northstar Analytics reimburses and the evidence, approval, and timing needed for payment. It supports reasonable spending and consistent financial records.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers expenses paid personally for an approved business purpose. Corporate-card transactions must also follow the documentation and approval rules. Personal purchases, commuting, and expenses outside an approved business purpose are not reimbursable unless an exception is recorded.

## 3. Definitions

- **Business expense** means a reasonable cost incurred for an authorized Northstar business purpose.
- **Receipt** means an itemized merchant record showing date, supplier, items or service, and amount.
- **Expense report** means the HR/Finance system submission containing expenses and supporting evidence.
- **Approver** means the employee's manager unless this policy assigns approval to Finance.

## 4. Policy Rules

### EX-001 — Reimbursable Expenses

Northstar may reimburse reasonable transportation, lodging, meals, supplies, and customer or team expenses when they have a documented business purpose and required approval. Reimbursement is not automatic merely because an expense is work-related.

### EX-002 — Receipt Threshold

Employees must attach an itemized receipt for each expense of $25 or more. For an expense below $25, the employee must still record the date, amount, merchant, and business purpose.

### EX-003 — Submission Deadline

Employees must submit expense reports within 10 business days after the end of the trip or, for non-travel expenses, within 10 business days after the expense occurs. Finance may return incomplete reports for correction.

### EX-004 — Manager Approval Threshold

Manager approval is required for every expense report. Finance approval is additionally required when the report total exceeds $5,000.

### EX-005 — Business Purpose

Each expense must state who benefited, the business activity, and the business purpose. A vague description such as "meeting" is insufficient when it does not identify the activity.

### EX-006 — Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Northstar must not reimburse personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, alcohol purchased for personal consumption, commuting between home and a regular worksite, or costs caused by deliberate policy bypass.

### EX-007 — Currency and Conversion

Employees must report amounts in the transaction currency and provide the conversion method when reimbursement is requested in a different currency. Finance determines the final converted amount using the recorded method.

## 5. Exceptions

Finance may grant an exception to the 10-business-day submission deadline for documented medical, travel, system, or other significant disruption. The employee must explain the disruption. Finance may also approve a missing receipt when the employee provides a signed declaration and alternate evidence; an exception does not make a non-reimbursable expense reimbursable.

## 6. Procedures

The employee enters each expense, attaches evidence, states the business purpose, and submits the report. The manager reviews business need and accuracy. Finance checks thresholds, documentation, and duplicate submissions before payment or return. Employees must not split one purchase into multiple reports to avoid a threshold.

## 7. Responsibilities

A report should allow a reviewer to reconstruct what was purchased, why it supported Northstar work, who approved it, and whether the evidence is complete. Employees should ask Finance before incurring an unusual cost when the reimbursement outcome is uncertain. Managers should return reports with a specific correction request rather than silently editing an employee's explanation. Finance may reject a report while preserving the employee's ability to resubmit corrected information.

Employees are responsible for accurate, timely reports and preserving receipts. Managers are responsible for reviewing every report. Finance is responsible for payment controls, exception records, and final review of reports over $5,000.
