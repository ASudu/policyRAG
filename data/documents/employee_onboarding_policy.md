## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum activities for bringing a new employee into Northstar Analytics. It coordinates documentation, training, access, and manager responsibilities so that work begins with appropriate controls.

## 2. Scope

It applies from accepted offer through the first 30 calendar days of employment. Transfers and rehires follow the same controls where relevant, with People Operations deciding which activities may be reused.

## 3. Definitions

- **Start date** means the first contracted working day recorded by People Operations.
- **Onboarding plan** means the checklist assigned to the employee, manager, and support teams.
- **Required training** means a course marked mandatory for the employee's role or worker type.
- **Least-privilege access** means access limited to the systems needed for assigned work.

## 4. Policy Rules

### ON-001 — Onboarding Plan

People Operations must create an onboarding plan before the employee's start date. The plan must identify the manager, role, start date, required training, and access dependencies.

### ON-002 — Employment Documentation

The employee must complete required employment and payroll documentation by the deadline provided by People Operations. People Operations must not grant final access when required identity or employment documentation is incomplete.

### ON-003 — Required Training

Employees must complete security, privacy, and code-of-conduct training within 10 business days of their start date. Role-specific training may have a different deadline stated in the onboarding plan.

### ON-004 — Access Provisioning

Managers must request least-privilege access before the start date when practicable. IT may provision access only after the request identifies the employee, role, systems, and approving manager.

### ON-005 — Access Review

Managers must review the new employee's initial access within 5 business days after the start date and request removal of access that is not needed.

### ON-006 — Orientation

The manager must provide role, team, communication, and safety orientation during the first 5 business days. Attendance may be remote when the employee's approved work arrangement is remote.

### ON-007 — Onboarding Records

People Operations must retain the completed onboarding plan and required completion records in the designated system. Employees must not store employment documents in personal storage.

### ON-008 — Completion Escalation

If required training or documentation is incomplete at its deadline, the manager must notify People Operations within 2 business days. Access may be restricted until the issue is resolved.

## 5. Exceptions

People Operations may approve a revised deadline for documented leave, delayed start, accessibility need, or system outage. The revised deadline and reason must be recorded. An exception to a training deadline does not authorize access to systems for which training is a prerequisite.

## 6. Procedures

People Operations opens the plan, the manager supplies role and access details, and IT provisions approved access. The employee completes documentation and training. The manager reviews access and orientation completion. People Operations closes the plan after required evidence is recorded.

## 7. Responsibilities

Onboarding tasks should be completed in the order that protects the employee and the company: documentation, role planning, controlled access, training, and confirmation. A manager should not ask IT for broad access simply to avoid a later request. The checklist is an accountability record, not a substitute for judgment about the employee's actual duties. People Operations may assign an owner when a task crosses team boundaries.

Employees complete documentation, training, and assigned tasks. Managers provide accurate requests, orientation, and access review. IT provisions and removes access. People Operations owns the plan, records, deadlines, and exceptions.
