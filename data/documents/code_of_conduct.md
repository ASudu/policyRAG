## 1. Purpose

The Code of Conduct describes the behavior expected when people work for or represent Northstar Analytics. It supports respectful collaboration, transparent decisions, protection of confidential information, and prompt reporting of concerns.

## 2. Scope

The code applies at work, at company-sponsored events, while traveling for business, and in work-related digital spaces. It applies to conduct toward coworkers, applicants, customers, suppliers, and other people affected by Northstar work.

## 3. Definitions

- **Conflict of interest** means a personal, financial, or relational interest that could improperly influence a Northstar decision.
- **Gift** means anything of value offered because of a business relationship, excluding ordinary low-value promotional items.
- **Retaliation** means an adverse action because a person raised a concern, participated in a review, or used a protected reporting channel.
- **Confidential information** means non-public Northstar, customer, employee, or partner information.

## 4. Policy Rules

### COC-001 — Respectful Workplace

Employees must treat others professionally and must not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, bullying, or unwelcome conduct. A person's intent does not excuse conduct that violates this rule.

### COC-002 — Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose an actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest to their manager and Ethics and Compliance before participating in the affected decision. Employees must follow the documented mitigation or recusal direction.

### COC-003 — Gifts and Hospitality

Employees must not offer or accept a gift or hospitality that could influence a business decision, create an obligation, or appear improper. Ordinary promotional items of nominal value may be accepted when they are infrequent and transparent.

### COC-004 — Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential information and may use it only for authorized Northstar business. Employees must not disclose confidential information to an unauthorized person or place it in an unapproved public service.

### COC-005 — Accurate Records

Employees must create and maintain truthful business records. Altering, concealing, or approving a record known to be materially inaccurate is prohibited.

### COC-006 — Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited. Managers must escalate suspected retaliation to Ethics and Compliance.

### COC-007 — Reporting Concerns

Employees must report suspected harassment, fraud, conflicts, confidentiality breaches, or other serious conduct concerns through the Ethics channel, manager, People Operations, or Incident Reporting Policy process.

## 5. Exceptions

Ethics and Compliance may provide a written interpretation for an unusual gift, conflict, or event. An interpretation may clarify whether conduct is permitted but cannot authorize harassment, retaliation, falsification, or unauthorized disclosure.

## 6. Procedures

An employee should preserve relevant facts and report a concern promptly. The receiving channel records the concern and routes it to the appropriate owner. Employees must cooperate honestly with an investigation and must not investigate allegations themselves when doing so could compromise evidence or fairness.

## 7. Responsibilities

People may raise concerns through more than one channel, and a person does not need to identify the final policy category before reporting. The receiving channel should focus on preserving facts, protecting people from retaliation, and routing the concern to the right owner. Confidentiality during a review is limited by the need to investigate and respond appropriately. A report made in good faith remains protected even when the concern is not substantiated.

Employees are responsible for professional conduct, disclosure, confidentiality, and reporting. Managers are responsible for modeling the code, escalating concerns, and preventing retaliation. Ethics and Compliance is responsible for interpretations, investigations, and maintaining reporting records.
