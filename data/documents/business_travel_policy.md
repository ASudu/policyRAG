## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes authorization and booking requirements for business travel. It defines reasonable transportation, lodging, and international-travel controls and coordinates travel expenses with the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## 2. Scope

It applies whenever a worker travels away from the approved work location for Northstar business. Personal extensions are allowed only when they create no additional company cost or risk and are disclosed to the manager.

## 3. Definitions

- **Business travel** means an approved trip whose primary purpose is Northstar work.
- **Travel authorization** means the recorded approval naming traveler, destination, dates, purpose, and estimated cost.
- **Standard airfare** means economy class or the lowest reasonable comparable fare.
- **International travel** means travel crossing a national border for company business.

## 4. Policy Rules

### TR-001 — Travel Authorization

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel. The authorization must include destination, dates, purpose, and estimated cost. A calendar invitation alone is not travel authorization.

### TR-002 — Booking Channel

Employees must book airfare and lodging through the Northstar travel portal or an approved travel agent. If the approved channel cannot provide a reasonable itinerary, Finance may authorize another channel in writing.

### TR-003 — Airfare Class

Employees must book standard airfare. Business class may be approved for a continuous flight segment longer than 8 hours when the manager documents the business reason and Finance approves the exception before booking.

### TR-004 — Lodging Limit

Lodging is reimbursable up to $300 per night before taxes and mandatory fees. A higher nightly rate requires documented destination constraints and Finance approval before booking when practicable.

### TR-005 — International Travel

International business travel requires manager and HR approval before booking. The traveler must confirm required work authorization and destination security guidance before departure; this policy does not prescribe a specific immigration document.

### TR-006 — Travel Changes

Employees must use reasonable efforts to cancel or change reservations promptly when plans change. Fees caused by a documented business change may be reimbursable; fees caused by an undisclosed personal change are not reimbursable.

### TR-007 — Travel Expense Submission

Travelers must submit related expenses within 10 business days after the trip ends under EX-003. The travel authorization number must be included in the report.

## 5. Exceptions

Finance may approve an exception to booking channel, lodging limit, or airfare class for safety, accessibility, availability, or documented business necessity. Emergency travel may be booked before approval, but the employee must notify the manager and Finance within 24 hours.

## 6. Procedures

The traveler creates an authorization request, obtains the required approvals, and books through the approved channel. Before departure, the traveler reviews destination guidance and confirms contact details. After the trip, the traveler submits an itemized report with the authorization number and receipts under EX-002.

## 7. Responsibilities

Travel decisions should consider safety, accessibility, cost, and the actual business purpose. The traveler should keep the authorization and booking record available throughout the trip and should promptly disclose a material change in destination or dates. Personal travel choices must remain separate from company-funded costs. When an itinerary cannot meet a traveler's documented accessibility need, the traveler should contact Finance before booking when practicable.

Travelers are responsible for authorization, reasonable booking, safety information, and timely reporting. Managers are responsible for purpose, cost, and business-need approval. HR reviews international work authorization. Finance owns booking exceptions, lodging controls, and reimbursement review.
