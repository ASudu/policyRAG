## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the conditions for working from home or from a location outside an employee's approved home state. It balances flexible work with tax, employment, security, collaboration, and business continuity requirements.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to employees with remote-eligible roles. A manager may require occasional onsite attendance for collaboration, equipment, training, or customer commitments. Remote work is a work arrangement, not a change to job classification or a promise of permanent location flexibility.

## 3. Definitions

- **Home-state remote work** means working from the employee's approved residential state.
- **Out-of-state remote work** means working from another U.S. state for any workday.
- **Rolling 12-month period** is measured backward from each proposed out-of-state workday.
- **Approved device** means a company-managed device configured under IS-008.

## 4. Policy Rules

### RW-001 — Home-State Remote Work

Employees may work remotely from their approved home state when their role and manager support remote work. Employees must maintain a suitable work environment and remain reachable during agreed working hours.

### RW-002 — Out-of-State Remote Work

Employees may work remotely from another U.S. state only with prior HR approval. The request must identify the state, dates, and work location before the employee begins working there.

### RW-003 — Duration Limit

Approved out-of-state remote work may not exceed 30 calendar days in a rolling 12-month period. The limit applies to the employee's total approved out-of-state remote days, not to each request separately.

### RW-004 — Approval Requirement

HR approval is required for every out-of-state request. Manager approval is also required when the requested period exceeds 10 consecutive business days. A manager approval does not replace HR approval.

### RW-005 — Tax and Employment Requirements

Employees are responsible for complying with applicable tax and employment requirements associated with an approved location. Northstar may deny a request when it cannot support the location's employment or payroll requirements.

### RW-006 — Availability and Work Hours

Employees must work the schedule agreed with their manager and must attend required meetings. Working from another time zone does not remove the availability requirement.

### RW-007 — Security and Equipment

Remote employees must use approved devices, approved storage, and authorized remote-access methods. Restricted data handled while traveling must follow DP-006 and IS-008.

## 5. Exceptions

HR may approve an exception to the location or duration rules for a documented business continuity event, emergency relocation, or other exceptional circumstance. The approval must state the location, dates, and any controls. An exception cannot waive information-security or data-privacy requirements.

## 6. Procedures

The employee submits the location and dates to the manager and HR before travel or relocation. HR checks employment feasibility and records its decision. The manager checks coverage, time-zone impact, and performance expectations. The employee confirms the approved location and uses the required device and access controls.

## 7. Responsibilities

Remote work arrangements must remain workable for the team and for the employee's assigned duties. A location change is not effective merely because an employee informs coworkers or updates a calendar. HR may ask for additional location details when needed to assess employment feasibility, but employees are not expected to invent tax or legal conclusions that the policy does not state. Decisions and exceptions should be retained with the request.

Employees are responsible for accurate location information, approvals, availability, and compliance obligations. Managers are responsible for coverage and work-expectation review. HR is responsible for location approval and records. IT Security is responsible for approved-device and remote-access controls.
