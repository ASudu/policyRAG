## 1. Purpose

This policy defines baseline safeguards for company devices, information, remote access, and security incidents. It assigns clear handling duties without assuming that every employee has the same access.

## 2. Scope

It applies to Northstar systems, company-managed devices, approved applications, and information processed for Northstar or its customers. More restrictive customer or project controls remain applicable when communicated to the worker.

## 3. Definitions

- **Company device** means a Northstar-managed laptop, phone, or other endpoint enrolled by IT.
- **Restricted data** means information labeled Restricted under the company data-classification scheme.
- **Approved storage** means Northstar-managed storage or another service listed by IT Security as approved for the data class.
- **Remote access** means access to an internal system from outside a Northstar-controlled network.

## 4. Policy Rules

### IS-001 — Company Devices

Employees must use company devices for company work unless IT Security has approved a documented exception. Employees must keep device-management controls active and must not disable required security software.

### IS-002 — Data Classification

Employees must handle data according to its assigned classification. When no classification is visible, the employee must treat non-public data as Internal until the data owner provides a classification.

### IS-003 — Approved Storage

Restricted data must be stored only in approved storage with access limited to authorized users. Restricted data must not be placed in personal cloud storage, removable media, or an unapproved collaboration service.

### IS-004 — Remote Access

Employees must use the approved remote-access method for internal systems. They must not bypass access controls, share remote-access sessions, or connect through an untrusted device.

### IS-005 — Public Networks

When using a public network, employees must use the company-approved secure connection before accessing internal systems. Employees should avoid discussing restricted data where unauthorized people can hear or view it.

### IS-006 — Least Privilege

Access must be limited to the systems and data needed for assigned work. Employees must not request, retain, or use access for a purpose outside that work.

### IS-007 — Device Loss

An employee who loses a company device or suspects unauthorized device access must report it through the Incident Reporting Policy within 24 hours. The employee must not wait to confirm whether data was accessed.

### IS-008 — Approved Device Requirement

Only IT-enrolled company devices with active encryption, endpoint protection, and current security updates are approved for Restricted data. IT Security maintains the approved-device inventory.

### IS-009 — Prohibited Practices

Employees must not bypass security controls, install unapproved software that handles company data, transmit passwords in plain text, or copy Restricted data to a personal account.

### IS-010 — Security Incidents

Suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, data exposure, or control bypass must be reported under IR-002 and IR-003. Employees must preserve relevant messages and system information when safe to do so.

## 5. Exceptions

IT Security may approve a time-limited exception for a documented business need after assessing risk and compensating controls. An exception must identify the system, data class, owner, expiration date, and controls. No employee may self-approve an exception.

## 6. Procedures

Employees use the service desk for device and access issues and the incident channel for suspected security events. IT enrolls devices, manages controls, reviews exceptions, and removes unnecessary access. Data owners classify information and approve authorized access.

## 7. Responsibilities

Security controls are intended to reduce risk without shifting classification decisions to individual guesswork. Employees should pause and ask the data owner or service desk when a label, application, or access path is unclear. IT Security may disable access or isolate a device during response. Managers and data owners must support timely access reviews, especially when duties or projects change.

Employees protect devices, follow access controls, and report concerns. Managers confirm business need and sponsor access. IT Security maintains standards, inventory, monitoring, and exceptions. Data owners classify information and review access.
