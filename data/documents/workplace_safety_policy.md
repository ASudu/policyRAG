## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Northstar Analytics prevents workplace harm and responds to unsafe conditions, emergencies, and injuries. It applies practical responsibilities to people working onsite or at an approved company event.

## 2. Scope

It covers Northstar offices, approved work areas, company events, and work activities performed under Northstar direction. It does not replace emergency services or location-specific instructions.

## 3. Definitions

- **Hazardous condition** means a condition or activity that could cause injury, illness, property damage, or unsafe exposure.
- **Safety incident** means an injury, near miss, property damage event, or hazardous-condition event connected to work.
- **Emergency** means an immediate threat requiring evacuation, shelter, medical response, or emergency services.
- **Safety lead** means the person assigned to coordinate local safety response.

## 4. Policy Rules

### WS-001 — Employee Safety Duty

Employees must follow posted safety instructions, use required protective equipment, and stop work when continuing would create an immediate serious risk. Employees should report hazards even when no injury occurred.

### WS-002 — Safety Orientation

Employees must complete workplace safety orientation within 5 business days of their start date and before performing work with a known elevated hazard. Managers must identify role-specific safety requirements.

### WS-003 — Hazard Reporting

Employees must report a hazardous condition to their manager or safety lead as soon as practical. They must not remove, repair, or investigate a hazard beyond their training.

### WS-004 — Injury and Near-Miss Reporting

Work-related injuries, near misses, and property damage must be reported under IR-001 within 24 hours of discovery. Medical emergencies must be addressed first; reporting follows when the person is safe.

### WS-005 — Emergency Procedure

In an emergency, employees must follow evacuation or shelter instructions, call local emergency services when needed, and report to the designated assembly point when safe. Employees must not re-enter until authorized.

### WS-006 — Manager Response

Managers must secure the area when safe, confirm affected workers receive help, preserve relevant information, and notify Workplace Operations within 24 hours of a report.

### WS-007 — Remote Work Safety

Employees working from home must maintain a reasonably safe work area and report work-related injuries or hazards under IR-001. Northstar does not require employees to disclose unrelated household conditions.

## 5. Exceptions

A safety lead may change an evacuation or reporting procedure during an emergency when following the standard procedure would increase immediate risk. The reason and actions taken must be documented after the emergency. No exception permits unsafe re-entry.

## 6. Procedures

The employee moves away from immediate danger, obtains emergency help, and reports the event. The manager or safety lead secures the area and preserves information. Workplace Operations records the incident, coordinates follow-up, and tracks corrective actions.

## 7. Responsibilities

Safety reporting is intended to improve conditions, not to assign blame for an honest report. Employees should give the clearest facts they have and identify immediate risks, even when they do not know the cause. Managers should make reasonable interim controls while awaiting a specialist response. Workplace Operations may request follow-up information, photographs, or witness details when doing so does not create additional risk.

Employees follow instructions, use equipment, and report hazards. Managers provide safe work direction and timely escalation. Safety leads coordinate local response. Workplace Operations maintains orientation, records, and corrective-action tracking.
